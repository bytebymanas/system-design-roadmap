45-minute design framework

Estimation cheat sheet

  • One day ≈ 86,400 seconds; use 10⁵ for quick math.
  • 10 million requests/day ≈ 100 QPS average; plan for a peak of 2–5× average.
  • 1 million writes/day at 1 KB ≈ 1 GB/day or 365 GB/year before replication.
  • Multiply stored data by the replication factor (commonly 3).
  • Latency anchors: RAM ≈ 100 ns; NVMe read ≈ 100 µs; same-datacenter round trip ≈ 0.5 ms; cross-continent ≈ 100–150 ms.

Common mistakes to eliminate

  • Jumping to boxes before clarifying requirements.
  • Naming technologies without explaining why.
  • Ignoring the write path or failure path.
  • Claiming exactly-once or strong consistency without discussing cost.
  • Spending 30 minutes on the high-level diagram and leaving no time for depth.

Read with a purpose

Resource shelf

Use books to build the mental model, then return to the roadmap and prove it with a blank-page design. Optional advanced material is deliberately separated.

Habits that beat passive reading

  • Redraw each design from memory one week later.
  • Keep a mistake log from every mock.
  • Explain one concept aloud each day as if teaching it.
  • Use optional items only after the core phase is stable; if you fall more than two weeks behind, skip optional topics.
